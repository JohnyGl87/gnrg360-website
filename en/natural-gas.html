Natural gas officer and engineering support for industrial plants

I am a mechanical engineer, a certified Ministry of Labor examiner and DVGW-certified. I offer to serve as a natural gas officer appointed by the plant.

The service is for plants converting to natural gas or already using it. I coordinate the plant's work with contractors, the distribution company and the Natural Gas Authority. The service is available throughout Israel.

What the service includes

  • Coordination and oversight on the plant's behalf of installation and maintenance contractors' work: schedules, conformity with the design and documentation.
  • Support for conversion to natural gas. From the initial review, through design and execution, to start-up.
  • Liaison with the distribution company and the Natural Gas Authority. Coordination, documents and tracking requirements.
  • Supplementary staff training. Knowing the system, operating it safely and what to do when something goes wrong. It supplements the operator training the law requires; it does not replace it.

The role is to represent and coordinate on the plant's behalf. Licensed work, such as supervising a gas installation, is done by license holders.

What Israeli law requires of a plant using natural gas

The subject is governed by the Gas (Safety and Licensing) Law, 1989 and its regulations. Key points:

  • Owner and holder responsibility. The owner and the holder of a natural gas consumer installation must keep and operate it properly and safely.
  • Compliance certificate. A consumer installation may not be installed or operated on natural gas without a compliance certificate from an inspection body. The certificate is valid for 5 years; the Minister may change this by order.
  • Operator training. Anyone doing operation work on a consumer installation must first receive safe-use training from the holder. Above 16 m³/h, the trainer must be the manufacturer, its representative, or an employee with more than one year of operating experience. This applies from July 1, 2028.

In the gas regulations, "the Commissioner" means the safety officer appointed under the Natural Gas Sector Law. The role I offer is a plant-appointed one, under the plant's own appointment.

The plant's safety officer

A 2015 directive of the Ministry of Labor's Occupational Safety and Health Administration covers safety officers at plants connected to natural gas. Under the directive, they must hold a certificate from a natural gas course.

Licensing reform for natural gas workers (2027–2028)

New regulations on licensing natural gas work were published in March 2026:

  • They come into force on January 1, 2027. The license requirement and the training and operation duties apply from July 1, 2028.
  • They set out license types, including licensed natural gas worker, licensed natural gas worker in buildings, installation inspectors and designers.
  • The Ministry of Labor sets the exams.
  • Some holders of existing Ministry of Labor certificates can apply through a transition route. This covers holders of the Natural Gas Facility Installer and Natural Gas Facility Operator, Level 1 certificates.

For a plant, this means checking in advance that its contractors and employees will hold the right license in time.

Converting to natural gas: the steps

  1. Suitability check. Which equipment can switch to gas, and is it economically worthwhile?
  2. Design and coordination. Working with the designer and the distribution company.
  3. Execution. Coordination and oversight on the plant's behalf of contractors, schedule and quality.
  4. Inspection and start-up. A compliance certificate before operation, and staff training.

FAQ

Does Israeli law require a plant to appoint a natural gas officer?

No. The Gas Law does not require such an appointment. Responsibility lies with the owner and the holder.

How long is a compliance certificate valid?

Five years, unless the Minister sets a different period by order.

When does the new license requirement apply?

July 1, 2028. The regulations themselves come into force on January 1, 2027.

What is DVGW?

A German technical and scientific association for gas and water that issues professional certifications. A DVGW certification is not an Israeli license.
